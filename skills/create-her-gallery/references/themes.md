# Visual directions

Choose the interaction layout before the palette. Use `romantic-stage` for a self-playing, page-by-page story; `classic-card` for a simple archival card; and `editorial-scroll` for an exhibition meant to be browsed by scrolling. Offer the directions below as starting points, not as limits. Base the final choice on the recipient's taste, background image, and emotional register of the material.

The accepted `romantic-stage` treatment uses a visible full-screen background, a milky translucent card without background blur, deep green-gray text, warm gold details, and a 2:3 media window. Preserve its interaction grammar even when adapting its palette. See [romantic-stage.md](romantic-stage.md).

## Moonlit Letter — 月下花笺

Indigo paper, silver text, plum highlights, and a faint luminous binding thread. Quiet, intimate, and suited to letters, night memories, and restrained poetry.

## Summer Film — 夏日胶片

Sunlit yellow, clear cobalt, tomato-red details, and film-frame crops. Lively and candid, suited to travel, friendship, jokes, and abundant everyday photos.

## Forest Specimen — 林间标本

Deep moss, mineral white, faded violet, and botanical labels. Reflective and tactile, suited to nature, craft, family archives, and long spans of time.

## Cosmic Stage — 星河剧场

Midnight navy, electric violet, pale blue, and orbital chapter marks. Expansive and cinematic, suited to ambition, transformation, performance, and many parallel identities.

## Custom direction

When none fits, derive a new direction from concrete personal material: a room, garment, handwriting style, landscape, object, or recurring color. Do not create another generic palette with only renamed colors.
